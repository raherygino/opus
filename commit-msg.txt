Fix transparent reintegration dialog background on Android

The Material3 AlertDialog was rendering with a transparent/missing
background in the app's custom theme. Replaced AlertDialog with a
Dialog + Surface组合 that explicitly sets:
  - color = MaterialTheme.colorScheme.surface
  - tonalElevation = 6.dp
  - shadowElevation = 8.dp
  - shape = RoundedCornerShape(20.dp)

This gives the dialog a solid, themed background with proper elevation
and rounded corners, regardless of the theme's AlertDialog defaults.
