Add GPS location capture to Armement (required on mobile)

On Android, the agent must now enable location services and capture
the device's GPS coordinates before an armement perception can be
created. The save is blocked until a location is captured. Desktop
does not capture location (no GPS hardware) — the fields are nullable
so desktop-created armements remain valid.
