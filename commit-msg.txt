Replace reintegration dialog with full-screen form using date/time pickers

The dialog-based reintegration form couldn't use native date and time
pickers comfortably. Replaced it with a dedicated full-screen
ArmementReintegrationScreen navigated to via a new route, matching
the pattern used by other form screens (Passation, Correspondance).

New files:
- OpusTimePickerDialog.kt — Material 3 TimePicker wrapped in an
  opaque Surface (mirrors OpusDatePickerDialog style)
- ArmementReintegrationScreen.kt — full-screen Scaffold with:
  - Weapon info card (read-only summary of the perception)
  - Date field → opens OpusDatePickerDialog
  - Time field → opens OpusTimePickerDialog
  - État and munitions text fields
  - GPS capture section (required on Android) with permission
    launcher, capture button, and clear button
  - GradientButton submit
- ArmementReintegrationViewModel.kt — HiltViewModel that loads the
  armement, manages form state, captures location via
  FusedLocationProvider, validates, and submits ReintegrationData

Navigation:
- New route MainRoutes.ArmementReintegration
  (sed_armement_reintegration/{armementId})
- Registered in MainScreen with slide transitions matching other
  form screens
- ArmementDetailScreen and ArmementScreen now navigate to this
  route instead of showing a dialog

Cleanup:
- Deleted ArmementReintegrationDialog.kt
- Removed unused reintegration state and methods from
  ArmementDetailViewModel and ArmementViewModel
- Removed unused ReintegrationData imports
